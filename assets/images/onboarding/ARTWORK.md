# Onboarding book artwork

The current book uses Notes, Diary, AI, Flow, People, Appearance, and Sync. See
[current artwork and final prompts](CURRENT_ARTWORK.md) for the replacement
spreads and Sync chapter. Tasks, Birthdays, and Movies below document the retired source artwork.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Seven full 4:3 spreads (two 2:3 leaves each) and a 4:5 cover. The WebP and JPEG files are the production assets; the pages include their paper texture, lettering, illustrations and static shadows. The app adds the book geometry, page curl, moving lighting and the editable cover name.

Headings and descriptions remain available through Flutter semantics. Text inside the artwork does not reflow with system text size.

## Depth materials

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. `paper-edge.webp` is a horizontal paper-cut texture; `binding-cloth.webp` is vertical lavender linen with an ivory seam. The app maps these to the page block and hinge, with dynamic lighting and a beveled cover. Production textures are downscaled to 1024 and 192 pixels wide respectively.

## Ink background

`ink-paper.webp` is a static 1024 × 1536 texture of aged ivory paper with sparse, faint dried ink drops. Generated with the built-in image generation tool and encoded as WebP at quality 88. `OnboardingInkBackground` uses `BoxFit.cover` with right alignment to keep the small margin drops visible on tall screens. The surface has no animation or shader clock; dark plum interface text keeps it readable.
